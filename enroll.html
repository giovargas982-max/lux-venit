Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Is the parent information meeting really required?

Yes — a parent information meeting is mandatory before you can register. Zoom attendance is available if you cannot come in person. Email us with your nearest location of interest and the age/gender of your singer(s) to receive the link.

How do I pay tuition?

You may pay by cash in an envelope, personal check payable to "Lux Venit Choral Academy," PayPal, Venmo, or Zelle. Tuition is due monthly.

Is there a limit on tuition for large families?

Yes! The family tuition cap is $100 per month regardless of how many children are enrolled. Supply fees and the building/insurance fee ($60 per family per semester) are separate.

Do I need a referral code or discount?

If a friend refers you and enrolls, you receive $40 off one month's tuition. Likewise, if you refer a family who enrolls, you receive $40 off.

What if we need to drop out?

Please notify the director as soon as possible. A prorated refund may be available depending on the circumstances.

Is an audition required?

No audition is required for Lower Elementary, Upper Elementary, or Men's & Ladies' Choirs. Only Chorale (9th–12th grade) requires an audition.

What kind of music will my child sing?

Baroque, Classical, Renaissance, folk songs, hymns, spirituals, and sacred and secular pieces in a variety of languages. The repertoire is chosen to glorify God while exposing students to diverse musical traditions.

What is the dress code for class?

Skirts and dresses must be below the knee. Shorts should be just above the knee or below. No spaghetti straps, tank tops, low-cut shirts, or inappropriate words/pictures on shirts.

How many absences are allowed?

A maximum of 2 absences per semester from regular class. Please notify the director in advance if your child will be absent.

Can we join mid-year?

Yes — students may join after each concert (approximately end of semester). Contact the director for the next available enrollment date.

Do parents need to stay during class?

Parents of K–2nd grade students must remain on campus. For 3rd–12th grade, it is at the parent's discretion.

Which homeschool co-ops are you affiliated with?

Lux Venit is open to students from all co-ops and homeschool associations, including CC, Classical Conversations, DE, Dominion Equippers, MGA, Morning Glory Academy, Kingdom Kids, NICHE, WACHE, Center for Home Education, Sonlight, MC2, Legacy, and Venture.
